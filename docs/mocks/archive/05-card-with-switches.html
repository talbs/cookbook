Step notes

  1. 1

    5 to 10 seconds on low. Stop the moment the flour disappears.

  2. 2

    Press plastic wrap against the dough. It keeps up to 72 hours.

  3. 3

    6 to 9 mounds per sheet. Turn any chocolate that pokes up flat. It makes a better-looking cookie.

  4. 4

    Surface cracked, edges golden, center still soft.

Notes

The original calls for 3.5 oz cookies. 2 oz yields far more, and they're still large with every texture you want.
